Privacy Policy

Crew Guide — last updated 26 October 2026

1. Who we are

Crew Guide is a mobile application providing operational ground information for flight crews — airports, handling, customs, transport, accommodation and catering. It is aimed at airline, business and general aviation crews.

Data controller: the publisher of Crew Guide, a company being incorporated in Hong Kong; its name and registered address will be added here. Contact: contact@crewguide.aero.

3. Health-related data

Some customs forms — notably disinsection and international health certificates — contain health-related information. Under Article 9 GDPR this is a special category of personal data. We only process it on your explicit consent, given when you fill in the form, and only to produce the document you asked for. You can delete these declarations at any time from the app.

4. What stays on your phone

  • Passport scanning is performed entirely on your device (Apple Vision). The photograph of your passport is never uploaded, never transmitted and never stored on our servers. Only the fields you choose to keep are saved.
  • Your personal customs notes are stored locally on the device and are not sent to our servers.

5. Where your data is stored

Our database and authentication are operated by Supabase, in a data centre located in Tokyo, Japan. If you are in the European Economic Area, this is a transfer outside the EEA. Japan benefits from an adequacy decision adopted by the European Commission on 23 January 2019, which recognises that it offers a level of protection essentially equivalent to EU law. No further safeguards are therefore required for this transfer.

7. What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use it for advertising, and the app contains no advertising or third-party tracking SDK.
  • We do not build behavioural profiles.

8. How long we keep it

  • Account data: as long as your account exists.
  • Customs and health declarations, catering orders: until you delete them, or until you delete your account.
  • Published contributions: they remain in the shared database after you delete your account, but are detached from your identity (see below).

9. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time, from the app: Profile → Delete my account. No email, no form, no waiting.

This permanently deletes your profile and username, your reviews, your customs and health declarations, your catering orders and any contribution still awaiting review.

Contributions that have already been published — customs records, business contacts and photos — remain available to other crews, with the link to you removed. They have become part of the shared database other crews rely on; deleting them would degrade the service for everyone. Once detached, they no longer identify you.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to your data, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing. Write to contact@crewguide.aero. Most of these you can exercise directly in the app — your profile is editable and account deletion is immediate.

If you are in France and believe your rights are not respected, you may lodge a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr); elsewhere in the EEA, with your national supervisory authority.

11. Security

Access to the database is enforced by row-level security policies: your declarations, orders and personal notes are readable only by your own account. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never visible to us. Traffic is encrypted in transit.

12. Children

Crew Guide is a professional tool for flight crews. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

13. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and announce any significant change in the app.
